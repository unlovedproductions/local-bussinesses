# Sell voice agents without a live demo

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Product | AI phone receptionist (Retell + Cal.com) |
| Problem | Live Retell web-call needs mic, login, credit, and attention — most owners won’t sit through it on a cold call or walk-in |
| Goal | Book a paid pilot / discovery visit using **async proof** + a clear next step |
| Honesty | Still do **not** sell emergency dispatch, cancel/reschedule, or live ERPNext as shipped |

## Reality check (what you have today)

Verified demo path is a **browser web call** in Retell + real Cal.com bookings (`voice-agent/DEMO.md`). There is **no public phone number** for prospects to dial, and no shipped leave-behind video in this repo.

So “no live demo” means one of:

1. **Sell the outcome with a story + paper/phone proof** (usable today), or  
2. **Pre-record one 90-second proof once**, then reuse it everywhere (best next asset).

Do not pretend the live web call is happening if it isn’t.

---

## The sell frame (use on every business)

Owners don’t buy “AI.” They buy **calls that stop dying**.

| They feel | You say |
|---|---|
| Phone rings mid-service | “The agent answers so you don’t put down the dog / wrench / patient.” |
| They already book online | “Online is for planners. Callers still call. That’s the hole.” |
| They’re closed part of the week | “Closed days are unpaid ads. The agent takes the booking or the lead.” |
| New clients must call | “Your policy is right. Automate the intake so the policy doesn’t cost you jobs.” |
| They want a price first | “Pilot is scoped after we map your phone + calendar. First I show what it does in 90 seconds — recording or in person — then quote.” |

**Close without a live demo:**  
> “I’m not asking you to sit through a tech demo right now. I’ll leave / text you a 90-second recording of the agent booking a real calendar slot. If that looks like your phone problem, we do a 20-minute setup visit and a paid pilot. Fair?”

If you don’t have the recording yet, substitute:  
> “I’ll text you a one-page that shows exactly what the agent says and what lands on the calendar. If that matches how your phone works, we schedule a setup visit — not a sales webinar.”

### B. Usable today with no new assets

1. **Phone-on-counter storyboard** — you narrate the 7 beats while they look at a printed Cal.com screenshot (or your phone photos of a past demo booking).  
2. **One-page leave-behind** — problem → what the agent does → what you never claim → next step (template below).  
3. **Their own phone test (no AI)** — ask them to miss the next call on purpose or check voicemail count. Sell the pain they already have.  
4. **Callback number offer** — “Text me ‘DEMO’ and I’ll send the recording / one-pager.” Converts tire-kickers without a mic session.

### C. Do not use as proof

- Live Retell dashboard on *their* Wi‑Fi mid-groom  
- Promising a dial-in number you don’t have yet  
- Claiming their Square/Moego/Groomer.io will “just connect” without a setup visit

## Pitch by business type (no live demo)

### 1. Groomer with online booking already  
*(Pawsitive, DashHound, Going Mutts / Square)*

**Open:** “I’m not here to replace your online booking. I’m here for the people who still call while you’re mid-groom.”  

**Proof:** Recording or storyboard of book + “calendar full → lead.”  

**Close:** “I’ll text the 90-second clip. If it’s your problem, we do a setup visit — keep Square/Moego, add the phone layer.”

### 2. Groomer where new clients must call  
*(Doggie Do’s)*

**Open:** “Your new clients have to call — that’s good intake and a busy line. We automate that first call so you don’t lose them to voicemail.”  

**Proof:** Storyboard of new-customer questions → slot offered → book or lead.  

**Close:** “Read the one-pager. If the intake list matches yours, we map your questions on the setup visit — no live demo required to say yes to that visit.”

### 3. Appointment shop with closed days  
*(PetStyling — Fri/Sat closed pattern)*

**Open:** “When you’re closed, good clients still call. Right now that’s voicemail. The agent books or takes a lead for the next open day.”  

**Proof:** Recording of after-hours / closed-day style request (or narrate it).  

**Close:** “I’ll leave the page. Text me after you listen to the clip if you want the setup visit on a day you’re open.”

### 4. Vet / dental (appointment offices)

**Open:** “Front desk can’t answer every line. We put an AI on overflow that checks availability and books new-patient or existing appointments you allow — or takes a callback lead.”  

**Proof:** Same booking recording; say clearly it’s a **grooming demo vertical** and their scripts/calendar get configured on setup.  

**Close:** “No live demo today — watch the clip, then we meet with whoever owns phones and the schedule.”  

**Never:** Claim EHR integrations you don’t have.

### 5. HVAC / plumbing (service trades)

**Open:** “After-hours and overflow calls are jobs or they’re voicemail. We answer, take the job details, and book a slot or log a lead. Dispatch stays with you.”  

**Proof:** Lead-capture beat in the recording (full calendar path).  

**Close:** “I’ll send the clip. Pilot is missed-call capture + booking — not a promise we roll trucks.”  

**Never:** Sell emergency dispatch as shipped.

## In-person walk-in (90 seconds, no laptop demo)

1. Card / one-pager on the counter.  
2. “Who owns phones?”  
3. One pain question: “About how many calls go to voicemail on a busy day?”  
4. Narrate three beats: answer → check calendar → book or lead.  
5. Show phone photo of Cal.com confirmation if you have it.  
6. Close: “I’ll text the recording. If it’s right, we do a 20-minute setup visit — not a Zoom theater.”  
7. Get a **cell number** before you leave. No number = no deal.
