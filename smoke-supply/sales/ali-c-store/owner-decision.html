What this will not do

No promised ranking. No promised number of new customers. Nobody honest can sell you “#1 on Google” or “30 new customers a month.” What you can judge is whether the work got done, and whether the monthly numbers move compared with a screenshot we take before launch.

Google and Facebook heavily restrict tobacco and vape ads. This package does not buy ads. It fixes the free listing and gives you a site you control.

The website will not list products California does not allow you to sell, and it will say the store is 21+. You confirm what is actually on your shelves before any product text is written.
