Most of a combined job is done in about 3–4 weeks. Local work needs you in the store once. Google sometimes verifies by video that day, and sometimes by a postcard.

What you have to do

  • If you buy local work: about 30–40 minutes in the store to confirm hours, the public phone number, and what you legally sell, then verify Google and let me photograph the store. The listing stays on an email you control. I am a manager. You can remove me.
  • If you buy Get Chosen: put the review card on the counter and ask customers.
  • If you buy a website: confirm what the pages should say. Pay half the combined setup before work starts. The other half is due when everything you marked is finished.

What this will not do

No promised ranking. No promised number of new customers. Nobody honest can sell you “#1 on Google” or “30 new customers a month.” What you can judge is whether the work got done, and whether the monthly numbers move compared with a screenshot we take before launch.

Google and Facebook heavily restrict tobacco and vape ads. This does not buy ads.

A website will not list products California does not allow you to sell, and it will say the store is 21+. You confirm what is on the shelves before any product text is written. If you buy no website, nothing about your products is published.

What you keep if you stop

  • If you bought a website: the domain stays in your name, and you keep the site files.
  • If you bought local work: the Google listing stays yours. Removing me does not delete it.
  • Any monthly fee runs for 3 months, then you can stop. Setup is not refunded once that half of the work is delivered.
