# Sales Dashboard & Owner View

**Working title:** What the Owner Can See From One Dashboard
**Duration target:** ~90 seconds
**Brand:** Smoke & Supply — fictional demo store
**Audience:** Independent local store owner
**Purpose:** Demonstrate how connected sales and inventory information could give the owner a clearer operational picture.

# CONTINUOUS VOICEOVER

Once your sales and inventory are connected, here's the kind of information you could have available from the owner's dashboard.

Instead of starting with individual transactions, the owner can start with a quick overview of what's happening across the store.

Sales can be separated into online and in-store activity, making it easier to see where today's transactions are coming from.

The owner can also see online orders and their status, giving them a quick way to monitor the pickup side of the business.

A top-sellers view can show which products are moving, giving the owner another piece of information to consider when managing inventory and placing orders.

The inventory view brings attention to products that may need attention, rather than making the owner search through every item manually.

When supported by the selected system, low-stock alerts could notify the owner when an item reaches a defined threshold.

Now the owner can look at sales activity alongside inventory information and see how products are moving through the store.

Instead of digging through different systems to figure out what needs attention, the dashboard can bring those items into one place.

The goal isn't simply to give the owner another dashboard. It's to make the information from the store's different sales channels easier to understand and act on.

This is what a connected setup could look like.

This is a simulated concept. Actual dashboard features and integrations depend on the systems selected for the store.
