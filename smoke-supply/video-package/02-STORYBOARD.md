# Storyboard — 9 Scenes + End Card

**Brand:** Smoke & Supply
**Format:** ~90-second simulated product demonstration
**Visual style:** Paper `#f6f1e8`, Forest `#2f4a3c`, Ember `#b54a2a`

Every UI frame should display a small:

**DEMO · SIMULATED**

badge.

## Title Card — 0–4s

**Visual:**

Dark forest background.

**Text:**

How POS & Inventory Integration Could Work

*Concept demo · Not a live POS recording*
