You are working inside a project containing a ZIP/package of assets, scripts, storyboards, visual guidelines, and supporting files for a fictional retail technology demonstration.

Your job is to inspect the entire project and turn the supplied materials into polished, professional demo videos that can be shown to a local independent store owner as part of a sales presentation.

Do NOT assume the existing files are perfectly organized. First inspect the entire project and determine what files, scripts, assets, and instructions are available.

These are NOT generic technology explainer videos.

The intended viewer is a busy local independent store owner.

The videos should communicate practical business value through demonstration rather than hype.

Prioritize:

**Clarity → Demonstration → Relevance → Confidence**

* Modern
* Clear

* Credible
* Slightly premium

Avoid:

* Excessive animation
* Corporate buzzwords
* Overly dramatic transitions
* Fake urgency
* Aggressive sales language
* Generic stock footage
* Flashy charts that don't communicate anything
* Unnecessary technical terminology
